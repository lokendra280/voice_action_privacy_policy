Welcome to VoiceActions ("VoiceActions", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the VoiceActions mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use VoiceActions, we may collect or process the following categories of information.

1.1 Account Information

If you create an account or sign in using a supported authentication provider, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Profile information provided by the authentication provider
  • Account identifier

1.2 Voice Recordings

VoiceActions allows you to record meetings, lectures, conversations, ideas, notes, and other audio content.

When you use the recording functionality, the application may process the audio recording to provide transcription, summaries, action-item extraction, reminders, and other AI-powered features.

Important: You should only record conversations when you have the appropriate permission or consent from other participants, where required by applicable laws.

1.3 Transcriptions and AI-Generated Content

Voice recordings may be converted into text using third-party speech-to-text or artificial intelligence services.

We may process the resulting text to generate:

  • Meeting summaries
  • Important points
  • Action items
  • Todos
  • Reminders
  • Decisions
  • Notes
  • Questions and answers
  • Other AI-generated insights

1.4 Todo and Reminder Information

When you create or allow VoiceActions to extract tasks, we may process information such as:

  • Task title
  • Task description
  • Due date
  • Reminder time
  • Priority
  • Completion status

2. Google Calendar Integration

VoiceActions may provide integration with Google Calendar. If you choose to connect your Google Calendar account, the application may access calendar information necessary to provide calendar-related features.

This may include:

  • Calendar event titles
  • Event descriptions
  • Event dates and times
  • Event locations
  • Calendar identifiers
  • Attendee information associated with calendar events

This information may be used to display your calendar, synchronize events, create calendar events, and schedule tasks based on your instructions.

VoiceActions does not use your Google Calendar information for advertising purposes.

You can revoke VoiceActions' access to your Google account at any time through your Google account settings.
